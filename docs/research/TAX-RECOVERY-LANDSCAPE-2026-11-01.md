# Tikdam: invoice tax recovery landscape

Research date: 1 November 2026, India time. Scope: purchase invoices AND invoices issued to clients. Target jurisdictions: India, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, United States, Indonesia (including Bali), Vietnam, Singapore, mainland China, United Arab Emirates (including Dubai), Japan.

This is source-linked route discovery for product planning. It is not a complete legal analysis, a verified rules dataset, or confirmation that a particular taxpayer qualifies. No calculator, treaty pair, rate table, deadline engine, or refund filing has been validated or implemented by this study. Links to older official material are research leads, not proof that every detail remains current. Unknown means research incomplete, not legally ineligible.

## Product finding

Tikdam can help someone recover value by finding a route, validating the documents and facts, distinguishing cash from credits or deductions, and preparing an action pack. A business registration certificate alone does not establish entitlement. Company incorporation, tax residence, VAT/GST registration, physical activity, invoice owner, place of supply, and the owner's personal residence are separate facts.

## Six different outcomes

| Outcome | What the user receives | What Tikdam must establish |
|---|---|---|
| Input-tax credit | A credit in a VAT/GST/consumption-tax return | Appropriate registration/regime, business use, taxable activity, valid documentation, exclusions and timing |
| Cash tax refund | A payment after a statutory claim or return | A refund provision, eligible claimant, refund period and net position, filing evidence and authority assessment |
| Income-tax deduction or capital allowance | Reduction of taxable profit, sometimes over time | Relevant taxpayer, business purpose, accounting/tax regime, capitalization, recoverable-tax interaction |
| Withholding credit/refund | Credit for tax taken from client payments; cash only if rules/final liability support it | Deduction certificate, remittance/statement matching, source rules, liable taxpayer and annual assessment |
| Treaty relief / foreign income-tax credit | Source-country reduction/refund or residence-country relief | Exact treaty, tax residence, income characterization, beneficial ownership where relevant, permanent establishment, treaty modifications and procedure |
| Supplier correction | Correct invoice, credit note, or repayment of wrongly charged tax | Why the charge was wrong, who must correct it, evidence of correction; not necessarily a tax-authority refund |

Tourist departure-shopping refunds are a separate route with their own claimant, goods, retailer, departure, personal-use and export conditions. Never substitute one for a foreign-business scheme. Exporting a product can also create import taxes at destination. Do not label an export-related refund as net savings without reviewing destination obligations.

## Client invoices: tax deducted from payments

Start with the gross invoice, indirect tax separately charged, amount actually paid, bank/platform fees and FX. A difference between invoice and cash received is not proof of withholding. Obtain the client's deduction statement and, where needed, evidence of remittance. A correctly withheld amount may be a final tax, an advance credit, or eligible for relief; it is not automatically refundable.

## Foreign-company and travelling-freelancer routing

Ask separately for the taxpayer who earns the income or owns the purchase, legal structure, incorporation country, claimed tax residence, VAT/GST registrations with regime/effective dates, place of business/establishments, owner residence, invoice recipient, purchase/supply country, place of work/use, invoice/payment/service dates, movement/import of goods and client country.

Example questions the product must answer without guessing:

- Indian freelancer buys a laptop in Singapore: was it invoiced to the freelancer or another company, is there Singapore GST registration, is a tourist route actually allowed, and what happens on import into India? An Indian GSTIN alone cannot answer these questions.
- UK business incurs UAE trade-fair costs: test FTA business-visitor rules, reciprocity and exclusions; do not send it through a UK domestic input-VAT calculation.
- India-based developer gets paid by a US client: place of work and income character matter; the customer's US address alone does not establish US-source service income. [US4]
- Canada-based business holds an asset used 40% commercially: investigate capital-property rules rather than assuming tax × 40% is its ITC. [CA2]
- A person's company is incorporated in one country while the owner lives/works elsewhere: keep both profiles distinct; unresolved residence or establishment is a review condition.

For withholding abroad, examine both source-country overpayment/treaty relief and residence-country foreign income-tax relief. These must be coordinated. For example, ATO explains that foreign income-tax offsets are limited and nonrefundable, and refunded foreign tax can affect eligibility. [AU5] Do not add a source refund and a residence-country credit for the same tax as independent cash recoveries. Income-tax treaties must not be used to establish VAT recovery.

## V1 product recommendation (proposal, not a scope reduction)

Keep all 11 target jurisdictions in the research and product catalogue. Readiness is specific to a route, taxpayer, date and scenario, not a flag saying an entire country is supported.

1. Purchase invoice review: extract and confirm facts, validate invoice completeness, find local ITC/foreign-business/supplier-correction or separate tourist route, identify deduction treatment and evidence gaps.
2. Client invoice review: reconcile gross invoice to payment, verify whether a deduction is tax, request certificate, identify credit/refund/treaty route and missing facts.
3. Claim preparation: a source-linked action pack with potential amount by benefit type, required documents, filing channel, verified deadline, person responsible and unresolved conditions.

For every target jurisdiction, release either a validated specific route or an explicit research/review response. It is misleading to market all countries as fully supported merely because the selector contains their names. Full V1 readiness means every advertised route has current rules, citations, exclusions, validated calculation tests and operational instructions. Broad route discovery can ship earlier than broad exact calculations, provided the coverage page says exactly which is available.

## Release blockers and research backlog

- Current primary legislation/amendments for India GST, Vietnam, China and Indonesia; country overviews and old consolidated PDFs are insufficient.
- Ordinary UAE domestic input-recovery rules; Japan nonresident taxable-business procedure; current normal/simplified nonresident registration limitations in AU/CA/SG.
- Each promised expense category: equipment, software/services, accommodation, transport, meals, vehicles, entertainment, imports and reverse charge have different rules. Prefer office equipment and ordinary software/services as initial research subjects; this is not yet a legal eligibility finding.
- Each treaty corridor: article, protocol, MLI modifications if relevant, dates, taxpayer, characterization, PE and relief procedure. No treaty corridor has been validated here.
- Withholding refund deadlines and forms for every advertised scenario. No broad automatic withholding calculator is ready.
- Subnational US states/localities, Canadian QST/PST and Indonesian/UAE local levies.
- Provider and hackathon market/eligibility terms, including ability to serve businesses in China. Research inclusion does not imply permission or technical availability.
- Native-language verification and qualified tax review of rules promoted to numerical decisions. This study does not replace that work.
