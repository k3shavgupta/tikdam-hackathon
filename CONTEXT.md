# Tikdam domain glossary

- **Taxpayer:** The individual or entity legally entitled to a tax credit, deduction, relief or refund. The owner of a company and the company are distinct taxpayers.
- **Incorporation jurisdiction:** Where a legal entity was formed; distinct from tax residence and places of business.
- **Tax residence:** A person's or entity's status under applicable domestic law and, when relevant, treaty provisions. Not inferred solely from nationality or incorporation.
- **Indirect-tax registration:** Registration in a VAT, GST, consumption-tax or equivalent regime, including its type and effective period.
- **Purchase invoice:** A supplier's document for goods or services acquired by the claimant.
- **Client invoice:** A document issued by the claimant for its sale or services.
- **Input-tax credit:** An amount allowed against indirect tax under a particular regime; not automatically a cash refund.
- **Cash refund:** Money repayable under an identified statutory or correction procedure, subject to the required facts and authority assessment.
- **Income-tax deduction:** An allowable reduction of taxable profit. Its amount is distinct from the resulting reduction in tax.
- **Capital allowance:** Relief for qualifying asset cost under applicable tax rules, potentially over time.
- **Withholding:** Tax deducted by a payer and accounted for under an applicable tax rule. A payment shortfall alone is not proof of withholding.
- **Treaty relief:** Relief available under a specified agreement for an eligible taxpayer, income type and period, subject to its conditions and procedures.
- **Foreign income-tax credit:** Residence-country relief for eligible foreign income tax, subject to domestic limits and interaction with source-country relief.
- **Supplier correction:** Correction or repayment by a supplier where a charge or document was wrong; distinct from a tax-authority refund.
- **Recovery route:** A defined legal/procedural path to a credit, refund, deduction, relief or correction for a specified scenario.
- **Coverage:** The routes, taxpayer types, periods and scenarios Tikdam has validated. A country appearing in a selector does not establish complete country coverage.
- **Unknown:** The relevant evidence or research is incomplete. Distinct from a verified finding of ineligibility.
- **Claim pack:** Documents, calculations, sources, conditions and next steps prepared for review or filing; creation does not establish that a claim was submitted or paid.
